01 / IMAGE-BASED SECURITY

An image. A location. A new approach.

A software concept that uses a specific digital image at a specific location as part of its password-protection process.

Rather than approaching password protection through conventional methods alone, this solution introduces an image-based element. The selected image is associated with an exact digital location, which may be hosted on a server or another accessible host.

The relationship between the selected resource and its location forms part of the intended protection mechanism. The exact implementation, supported environments and security properties depend on the final technical specification.

02 / HARDWARE-BASED SECURITY

Powerful thinking. Compact design.

A dedicated microchip integrated into a USB device, conceived to activate a computer-encryption process when connected.

This solution explores a direct connection between dedicated hardware and digital device protection. By integrating a specialized microchip into a USB form factor, the concept aims to make the initiation of encryption more straightforward.

Compatibility, authorization requirements, encryption scope, recovery procedures and supported operating systems must be defined and verified for the final product.
